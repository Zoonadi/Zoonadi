### Hey there! I’m Mayamiko,

I'm a data and digital health professional working at the intersection of DHIS2, data management, analytics, and data-driven decision-making.

I'm passionate about making data easier to understand. Whether that's designing better information systems, improving data quality, building dashboards, or breaking down technical concepts so others can learn and apply them.

Here on GitHub, I share DHIS2 resources, data tools, examples, and projects I've built or found useful along the way.

#### 🎯 What I Do
- **DHIS2 & Digital Health:** Designing, configuring, and working with health information systems and DHIS2 metadata.
- **Data Management:** Improving data quality, workflows, reporting processes, and information systems.
- **Data Analytics:** Turning data into useful insights through Power BI, Excel, SQL, and visualization.
- **Data Education:** Creating tutorials, technical articles, and practical resources that make complex concepts easier to understand.
- **Knowledge Sharing:** Sharing what I learn through the DHIS2 Community of Practice, YouTube, and other platforms.
